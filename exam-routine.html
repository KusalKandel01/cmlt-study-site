01 Source & Important Notice

These dates come directly from the official CTEVT exam routine PDF, published by the Exam Control Office, Sanothimi, Bhaktapur. The full notice covers dozens of programs — this page pulls out only the CMLT-relevant rows.

Practical rules that actually matter

  • No makeup exams. If you're absent for any reason, there's no second arrangement — the council is explicit about this.
  • Sit only at your assigned exam center. Center changes are not permitted.
  • Exams are not postponed for unexpected holidays without prior notice from the council — don't assume a holiday cancels an exam.
  • Log books / psychometric charts can only be taken into the exam hall with the invigilator's explicit permission.
  • Routine clash? If two of your subjects land on the same date, contact the Exam Control Office within 7 days of this notice's publication date.
  • Practical exams happen at your own school, must be completed within 15 days of the theory exam ending, with marks/attendance entered into ITMS online and submitted to the province office — after that window, late practical paperwork won't be accepted.
