03 How To Score Max From Zero

Nine subjects, zero background, 20 days — real mastery isn't possible, but maximizing marks is. CTEVT papers are highly patterned. Use that.

The exam structure (learn this Day 1)

Every CTEVT paper follows the same shape: Group A — very short answers, 1 mark each (definitions, one-word, fill-in) · Group B — short answers, 3–5 marks (short explanations) · Group C — long answers, 8–10 marks (essay-style, diagrams). Group A+B together are usually over half the total marks and are almost pure memorization.

The four rules for every subject

1. Definitions and diagrams first — fastest marks, need zero deep understanding.
2. Past papers ARE your syllabus — study what's actually been asked before the full notes.
3. VVI question sets over full chapters — someone already did the triage.
4. Partial understanding + a memorized model answer beats no answer, every time, in Group B/C.

Why "HA" labels show up on some sources

CTEVT publishes one shared curriculum — "Health Science First Year All" — used by every Health Science diploma program (HA, CMLT, Radiography, Pharmacy, Dental, Ophthalmic, and others). Your own official CMLT curriculum document confirms this directly: it says "See Separate Curriculum for Health Science First Year All" instead of listing its own Year 1 subjects. Your CMLT-specific coursework (Microbiology, Hematology, Biochemistry, Parasitology, etc.) only starts in Year 2. So any source labeled "HA First Year" for these 9 subjects applies to you too — confirmed independently by three sources, including ctevtnote.com's own CMLT section routing to the same pages.
