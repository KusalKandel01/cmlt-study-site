1 Unit 6: Genetics & Genetic Disorders (previously missing entirely)

Directly relevant to lab diagnostic work — recognizing these conditions is part of the job.

DisorderCause
Down's SyndromeExtra copy of chromosome 21 (trisomy)
Edward's SyndromeExtra copy of chromosome 18
Turner's SyndromeMissing/partial X chromosome (females)
Klinefelter's SyndromeExtra X chromosome in males (XXY)
AlbinismGene mutation affecting melanin production
HaemophiliaX-linked recessive; blood fails to clot properly

Mendel's Laws (foundation for all of the above)

  1. Law of Dominance — one allele masks the expression of the other in a heterozygote
  2. Law of Segregation — the two alleles for a trait separate during gamete formation
  3. Law of Independent Assortment — genes for different traits are inherited independently of each other

Mutation types: Gene mutation (substitution, frameshift, nonsense) vs Chromosomal mutation (aberration, ploidy).

3 Unit 5: Physiology (12 marks)

Five processes, each tested with "define + experiment + factors affecting":

ProcessCore definition
DiffusionMovement of particles from high to low concentration
OsmosisMovement of water across a semi-permeable membrane; osmotic pressure, plasmolysis
TranspirationWater loss via stomata; factors: light, humidity, wind, temperature
Photosynthesis6CO₂ + 6H₂O + light → C₆H₁₂O₆ + 6O₂; light-dependent + light-independent stages
RespirationAerobic (with O₂) vs anaerobic (without O₂)

4 Unit 3: Cell Biology (15 marks)

Cell wall, cell membrane (fluid mosaic model), organelles (mitochondria, plastids, ER, Golgi, ribosomes), nucleus, chromosomes. Cell division: Mitosis (growth/repair) vs Meiosis (gametes) — know the phase-by-phase differences, a very common Group B "differentiate between" question.

5 Remaining Units — Quick Reference

  • Life Components (5 marks): Carbohydrates, Lipids, Proteins, Enzymes, Nucleic acids (DNA double helix, RNA types)
  • Environmental Biology (8 marks): Ecosystem components, food chain/web, greenhouse effect, ozone depletion, acid rain
  • Economic Botany (4 marks): Named medicinal plants — Ginger, Cinnamon, Cardamom, Holy basil, Neem
  • Biotechnology (6 marks): Plant tissue culture, genetic engineering (insulin example), DNA fingerprinting, fermentation
