2 Plant Tissues

  • Meristematic tissue — actively dividing cells found at growing tips (roots, shoots); responsible for growth
  • Permanent tissue — cells that have stopped dividing and are specialized for a function (e.g. epidermis, xylem, phloem)

3 Photosynthesis (Group B/C favorite)

Definition: the process by which green plants make their own food using sunlight, carbon dioxide, and water, releasing oxygen as a byproduct.

6CO₂ + 6H₂O + sunlight → C₆H₁₂O₆ + 6O₂

Factors affecting photosynthesis:

  1. Sunlight intensity
  2. Carbon dioxide concentration
  3. Temperature
  4. Water availability

4 Respiration in Plants

Plants respire continuously (unlike photosynthesis, which only happens in light) — breaking down glucose to release energy, consuming oxygen and releasing carbon dioxide. This is the reverse of photosynthesis.

6 Transpiration

Definition: the loss of water vapor from plant parts (mainly leaves) through small pores called stomata. Helps in water transport, cooling the plant, and mineral absorption.

7 Group B Model — "Write the importance of photosynthesis"

  1. Produces food (glucose) for the plant and, indirectly, all living things
  2. Releases oxygen needed for respiration of all aerobic organisms
  3. Removes carbon dioxide from the atmosphere
  4. Forms the base of nearly every food chain
