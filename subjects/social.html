1 Physical Regions of Nepal

RegionKey feature
Himalayan RegionHighest region, includes Mt. Everest (8,848.86m)
Hill RegionMiddle region, includes the Kathmandu Valley
Terai RegionLowest, flattest, most fertile region

2 Federal Structure (high-frequency numbers)

  • 3 tiers of government: Federal, Provincial, Local
  • 7 provinces
  • 753 local levels (metropolitan, sub-metropolitan, municipality, rural municipality)
  • Constitution promulgated: 2072 BS (2015 AD)

3 Three Branches of Government

BranchFunction
LegislativeMakes laws (Parliament)
ExecutiveImplements laws (Council of Ministers)
JudiciaryInterprets laws, delivers justice (Courts)

4 Nepal's Unification — Key Facts

  • Prithvi Narayan Shah — King of Gorkha, began the unification campaign
  • Unification campaign began from the small kingdom of Gorkha
  • Kathmandu Valley was conquered in 1768 AD

5 Fundamental Rights (Group B favorite)

"List the fundamental rights guaranteed by the constitution" — give 5 as a numbered list:

  1. Right to live with dignity
  2. Right to equality
  3. Right to freedom
  4. Right to education
  5. Right to health

7 Group B Model — "Write the importance of the constitution"

  1. It is the fundamental/supreme law of the country
  2. Guarantees fundamental rights to citizens
  3. Defines the structure and powers of government
  4. Ensures rule of law and justice
