  • Learn the specific numbers cold (7 provinces, 753 local levels, 2072 BS constitution) — guaranteed Group A fill-in points.

1 Unit 1: History of Nepal (11 marks)

  • Ancient Nepal: Origin of the word "Nepal", Kathmandu Valley civilization, Malla period social reforms
  • Rana rule: cultural development, rise and fall of the Rana regime, education development under Rana rule
  • Democracy movements: first elected government (2015 BS), People's Movement of 2046 BS (1990 AD), Second People's Movement 2062/063 BS
  • Constitutions: comparison of the 2047 BS constitution vs the Federal Democratic Republic constitution of 2072 BS (2015 AD)

2 Unit 4: Politics & Government (11 marks)

  • 3 tiers of government: Federal, Provincial, Local
  • Federal Parliament: House of Representatives + National Assembly
  • Executive: Council of Ministers
  • Judiciary: Supreme Court → Appeal Court → District Court
  • Local level: Village/Municipal Assembly + District Coordination Committee

Group B model — "Write the fundamental rights guaranteed by the constitution"

  1. Right to live with dignity
  2. Right to equality
  3. Right to freedom
  4. Right to education
  5. Right to health

3 Unit 2: Society & Culture (9 marks)

Population growth factors (named explicitly in curriculum): fertility, mortality, migration.

Named social problems: Poverty, gender issues, unemployment, child labor, drug addiction, trafficking, brain drain.

National languages named: Nepali, Newari, Sanskrit, Maithili. Cultural heritage covers Himalayan, Hilly, and Terai regions — dress, festivals, music, and mountaineering/rafting/trekking as tourism topics.

4 Unit 3: Nepalese Economy (6 marks) & Unit 5: Foreign Policy (3 marks)

Economy: Affecting factors — poverty, inequality, population growth, unemployment. Features: agriculture/land reform, industrial development, trade, tourism.

Foreign policy features (named): Non-alignment, Panchsheel principles, acceptance of the UN Charter, regional cooperation. Relations specifically named with China and India.

5 Unit 1 (continued): Land, Education & Sub-topics

  • Geographical divisions: ecologic, climatic, river-based, vegetation-based, and administrative — Nepal can be divided along any of these five lenses
  • Educational development: traced from ancient to modern period; political and social factors shaping it; the distinct roles of technical/vocational vs general education — directly relevant given you're in a TVET program yourself

6 Unit 2 (continued): Tourism & Heritage

Cultural heritage spans food habits, dress and ornaments, festivals and temples, music/songs/dances, and traditional occupations across the Himalayan, Hilly, and Terai regions. Named tourism activities: mountaineering, rafting, trekking — Nepal's core tourism economy.
