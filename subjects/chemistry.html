Group B model — "What is pH? State its importance in the human body."

  1. pH is a measure of hydrogen ion concentration, indicating how acidic or basic a solution is (scale 0–14)
  2. Normal blood pH is maintained between 7.35–7.45
  3. Enzyme activity, protein structure, and cell function all depend on stable pH
  4. Buffer systems (e.g., bicarbonate buffer) resist pH changes in blood

Group B model — "Write the functions of proteins"

  1. Structural support (e.g., collagen)
  2. Enzymatic catalysis of biochemical reactions
  3. Transport (e.g., hemoglobin carries oxygen)
  4. Immune defense (antibodies)
  5. Made of amino acids joined by peptide bonds; can be denatured by heat or pH change

3 Unit 2: Inorganic & Environmental Chemistry (20 marks)

  • Hydrogen, Oxygen, Water: hard vs soft water, water treatment methods (Clark's process)
  • Metals & Minerals: biological importance of Na, K, Ca, Mg, Fe, Zn — directly relevant to lab chemistry panels
  • Pollution: named pollutants (CO₂, SO₂, O₃, CO, lead, CFCs), greenhouse effect, ozone depletion, acid rain
