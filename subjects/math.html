3 Sets Basics

A set is a well-defined collection of distinct objects. Key symbols: ∪ (union — combine both sets), ∩ (intersection — common elements only), ∅ (empty set), n(A) (number of elements in set A).

4 Trigonometry Basics

6 Measures of Dispersion

Standard deviation measures how spread out the data is from the mean — the most commonly tested dispersion measure. A low standard deviation means data points are close to the mean; a high one means they're spread out.

8 Worked Example — Mean

Model for how to show working (partial credit matters here even more than other subjects):

Q: Find the mean of 4, 8, 6, 5, 3, 2, 8, 5, 4, 7

Sum = 4+8+6+5+3+2+8+5+4+7 = 52
Number of values = 10
Mean = 52 ÷ 10 = 5.2
