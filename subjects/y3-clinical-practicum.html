CMLT Year 3

Clinical Practicum I, II & III

24 weeks of hospital/lab-based placement, evaluated by skills performance and viva — not a written exam like the theory subjects.

← Year 3

01 How This Differs From Theory Subjects

There's no written Group A/B/C exam here. Each practicum is evaluated by: institutional attendance (10%), logbook/practicum book maintenance (10%), spot performance — assigned task, identification, preparation, measurement (40%), and viva voce — internal examiner (20%) + external examiner (20%). Each block is worth 200 marks (100 internal + 100 final evaluation).

03 Clinical Practicum I — Microbiology & Parasitology (9 weeks)

Placement split: Microbiology Lab (5 weeks), Parasitology Lab (4 weeks). Skills you must be able to perform independently and accurately:

Bacteriology

  • Microscopy, sample collection, sterilization & safety precautions
  • Gram stain; AFB stain (TB/leprosy)
  • Urine, blood, pus, stool, throat swab, body fluid, and wound swab culture/sensitivity

Immunology / Serology

  • Widal test, RPR (VDRL), RA factor, ASO, CRP, Aldehyde test
  • HIV I-II antibody spot test, HBsAg spot test, Anti-HCV spot test, TPHA

Parasitology

  • Urine analysis — routine/microscopic, protein, glucose, specific gravity, bile salt/pigment, reducing substances, ketone, urine pregnancy test, chyle, urobilinogen, Benedict test, sulfosalicylic acid test
  • Stool examination — routine/microscopic, occult blood, reducing substances, concentration methods for ova/cysts, H. pylori antigen test

Miscellaneous

  • Semen analysis
  • KOH & germ tube test for fungal study
  • CSF/body fluid — cell count (TLC & DLC), sugar and protein estimation

04 Clinical Practicum II — Hematology & Blood Banking (9 weeks)

Full 9 weeks in a single Hematology & Blood Banking lab. Skills you must be able to perform independently and accurately:

  • TLC, DLC, ESR, PCV, hemoglobin estimation
  • Bleeding time, clotting time, PT, APTT
  • Platelet count, RBC count, reticulocyte count
  • Blood grouping (ABO), Rh typing, cross-matching, Coombs' tests
  • RBC indices (MCV, MCH, MCHC)
  • Hemoparasite identification (malaria parasite, microfilaria)

05 Clinical Practicum III — Biochemistry, Histopathology & Cytopathology (10 weeks)

Placement split: Biochemistry Lab (5 weeks), Histopathology & Cytopathology Lab (5 weeks). Skills you must be able to perform independently and accurately:

Biochemistry

Reagent preparation and estimation of: glucose, urea, creatinine, uric acid, total protein, albumin, A/G ratio, amylase, lipid profile, bilirubin, ALT (SGPT), AST (SGOT), alkaline phosphatase, sodium, potassium, chloride, calcium — plus preparing a standard graph for each test.

Histopathology & Cytopathology

  • Microscopy; fixative preparation and tissue fixation
  • Specimen collection, transport, and processing
  • Section cutting; reagent/stain preparation for H&E, PAS/Alcian blue, Ziehl-Neelsen
  • Reagent/stain preparation for Giemsa and Papanicolaou
  • Cytological fixation, smear preparation, PAP staining
  • FNAC smear staining (Giemsa & PAP); mounting stained smears/sections
  • Aceto-orcein staining for Barr bodies
