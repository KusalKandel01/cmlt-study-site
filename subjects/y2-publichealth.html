✓ What To Do / What To Skip

DO

  • First Aid (14 marks) is the single biggest unit and the most practically memorable — learn CAB/CPR and the named-emergency management list cold.
  • The epidemiology terminology list (epidemic/endemic/pandemic/sporadic etc.) is dense but extremely high-yield for Group A.
  • MCH and Waste Disposal are tied at 11 marks each — both reward straightforward memorization over deep understanding.

DON'T

  • Don't skip Health Education & Health Promotion (4 marks) entirely — it's short enough to fully cover in one pass.

  • Concept and definition of public health
  • Functions and goals of public health
  • Scope of public health and its approaches

Ch. 2 Basic Epidemiology (9 marks · 15 hrs)

Key terminology (very high Group A yield): isolation, quarantine, vaccine, zoonosis, epidemic, endemic, pandemic, sporadic, vector, host, reservoir, agent, incubation period, window period, incidence, surveillance, prevalence.

  • Epidemiological triad (agent-host-environment)
  • Epidemiological measurements
  • Dynamics of infectious disease transmission
  • Infection prevention and control measures
  • Immunity and its types — active vs passive immunization; National Immunization Programme
  • Screening of diseases — iceberg phenomenon, screening types and applications

Ch. 3 Nutrition (9 marks · 15 hrs)

Definition and concept of nutrition; nutrients; classification of food.

For protein, carbohydrates, fats, vitamins, and minerals: know definition, function, daily requirement, and deficiency disorder for each.

  • Concept and characteristics of a balanced diet
  • Malnutrition — definition, causes, types, prevention
  • Food security, food hygiene, food adulteration, food fortification, and preservation techniques

Ch. 4 Maternal and Child Health (MCH) (11 marks · 20 hrs)

  • Concept and scope of safe motherhood; ANC and PNC explained
  • Obstetric referral; concept, definition, scope of reproductive health
  • Breastfeeding
    • Definition and technique
    • Breast milk storage
    • Advantages of breastfeeding vs disadvantages of bottle feeding
    • Common problems related to breastfeeding
  • Concept of immunization and Nepal's current immunization schedule
  • Family planning — objectives and methods available in Nepal

Ch. 8 Waste Disposal System (11 marks · 20 hrs)

Biodegradable vs non-biodegradable solid waste. The 3R concept — reduce, reuse, recycle.

  • Waste disposal stages — collection, storage, transportation, ultimate disposal
  • Disposal methods — sanitary landfilling, dumping, composting, incineration, burial
  • Excreta disposal techniques
  • Vector control measures (mosquito- and rodent-borne diseases)

Hospital waste management

  • Hazards of hospital waste
  • Separation of waste, incineration, mercury management
  • WHO hospital waste management guideline
  • Sources and management of liquid waste

Ch. 9 First Aid (14 marks · 25 hrs)

The highest-marks unit in this subject. First aid treatment and its importance; basic qualities/responsibilities of a first aider.

Key terminology: casualty/victim, first aider, refer, trauma, antiseptic, immobilization, cyanosis, hemorrhage, stroke.

CAB (Circulation, Airway, Breathing) and the procedure of CPR — a classic Group C answer.

Medical emergencies — causes, symptoms & first aid management

  • Shock
  • Intoxication/poisoning (insecticides, rodenticides, drugs, alcohol)
  • Foreign bodies in ear, nose, throat, eyes
  • Road traffic accidents
  • Hemorrhage
  • Burns
  • Frostbite
  • Strokes
  • Acute mountain sickness
  • Broken bones and dislocation
  • Rabid animal bite
  • Snake and insect bite

Qn Practice Questions — Public Health and First Aid

Group A · 1 mark each
  1. Define epidemic and endemic.
  2. What does CAB stand for in CPR?
  3. Define quarantine.
  4. Name the 3R concept in waste management.
  5. Define window period.
Group B · 3–5 marks
  1. Differentiate between active and passive immunization.
  2. Explain the advantages of breastfeeding over bottle feeding.
  3. Describe the first aid management of shock.
Group C · 8–10 marks
  1. Describe the epidemiological triad and the dynamics of infectious disease transmission, with prevention and control measures.
  2. Discuss the causes, symptoms, and first aid management of any three named medical emergencies.
  3. Explain hospital waste management — hazards, separation, and disposal, according to WHO guidelines.
