2 Motion — Key Definitions

  • Speed — distance covered per unit time (scalar, direction doesn't matter)
  • Velocity — displacement per unit time (vector, direction matters)
  • Acceleration — rate of change of velocity

3 Equations of Motion

Three equations, asked constantly in numerical problems. u = initial velocity, v = final velocity, a = acceleration, t = time, s = distance.

EquationUse
v = u + atFind final velocity
s = ut + ½at²Find distance
v² = u² + 2asFind velocity without time

4 Newton's Laws of Motion (Group B favorite)

  1. First Law (Inertia) — An object stays at rest or in uniform motion unless acted on by an external force.
  2. Second Law — Force = mass × acceleration (F = ma).
  3. Third Law — For every action, there is an equal and opposite reaction.

6 Heat & Temperature

Heat is a form of energy that flows from a hotter body to a colder one. Temperature is the measure of how hot or cold a body is. Common conversion: °F = (°C × 9/5) + 32. Water boils at 100°C (212°F) and freezes at 0°C (32°F) at sea level.

7 Light Basics

  • Reflection — light bouncing back after hitting a surface (angle of incidence = angle of reflection)
  • Refraction — light bending as it passes from one medium to another (e.g. air to water)
