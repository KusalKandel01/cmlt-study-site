Group B model — "State Newton's three laws of motion"

  1. First Law (Inertia): An object stays at rest or in uniform motion unless acted on by an external force
  2. Second Law: Force = mass × acceleration (F = ma)
  3. Third Law: For every action, there is an equal and opposite reaction

Group B model — "Write the medical uses of radioactivity"

  1. Cancer treatment (radiotherapy) using controlled radiation to destroy malignant cells
  2. Diagnostic imaging using radioactive tracers
  3. Sterilization of medical equipment
  4. Radio-carbon dating (research application)

3 Electrostatics & Magnetism (9 marks each — previously skipped entirely)

  • Electrostatics: Coulomb's law, electric field, potential, capacitor and capacitance, electron volt
  • Magnetism: Magnetic moment, Coulomb's law for magnetism, terrestrial magnetism (dip, declination), dia/para/ferromagnetic properties

4 Current Electricity (11 marks)

Ohm's Law: V = I × R — the most-tested single formula. Also: series/parallel resistance combinations, Joule's laws of heating, Faraday's laws of electrolysis, AC vs DC.

5 Heat, Light, Waves & Sound — Quick Reference

  • Heat (9 marks): Thermometry, linear/cubical expansion, specific heat capacity, black body radiation — "medical uses of heat radiation (thermal therapy)" is explicitly named
  • Light (6 marks): Reflection/refraction laws, human eye structure and defects, microscope magnification
  • Waves & Sound (6 marks): Longitudinal vs transverse waves; ultrasonic waves and their medical uses are explicitly named — high relevance for a lab tech
